Pricing FAQ

Common questions

Do you bill hourly?

No. Every engagement has a fixed fee agreed in writing before work starts.

What does the smallest engagement cost?

Single workstreams start at $1,000, for example a comp set, a screening pass or a bottom-up market sizing.

What if the scope changes midway?

We flag it as soon as we see it and agree any change in writing before doing extra work. You will not receive an invoice you did not expect.

How does payment work?

Payment terms are set out in a short engagement letter before work begins. International bank transfer and common business payment platforms are supported.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most clients start with a single fixed-fee pilot workstream, usually delivered in one to two weeks, before committing to anything larger.

Is there a long-term commitment for monthly support?

No. Embedded support runs month to month with a defined scope, so you can scale up, down or pause as deal flow changes.
