Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what personal information HSM Strategic ("we", "us") collects through www.hsmstrategic.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. HSM Strategic is based in Lahore, Pakistan. Questions about this policy can be sent to mustafa@hsmstrategic.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you email us, message us on LinkedIn or WhatsApp, fill in the contact form or book a call, we receive the details you provide. This usually means your name, email address, firm and the content of your message.
  • Newsletter sign-ups. If you subscribe to Insights, we collect your email address so we can send you new articles.
  • Usage data. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may record basic technical information such as your browser type, the pages you visit, the referring site and an approximate location based on your IP address.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and to scope and deliver engagements
  • To send the Insights newsletter, if you subscribed
  • To understand how the site is used and to improve it
  • To meet legal, accounting and security obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Confidential deal information

Deal materials shared during an engagement are covered by the NDA and engagement terms agreed with that client, not by this website policy. They are used only for that engagement.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the site and our business. These include website hosting, email, form handling, scheduling, newsletter delivery and analytics providers. They may process data only on our behalf. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Cookies

The site may use a small number of cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work without them.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and correspondence records for as long as needed to respond and manage any resulting relationship, and to meet legal and accounting requirements. Newsletter subscribers are kept until they unsubscribe.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it for a particular purpose, by emailing mustafa@hsmstrategic.com. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law, such as the GDPR in the UK and EU. We will honor those rights where they apply.

International transfers

We are based in Pakistan, and our service providers may process data in other countries, including the United States. By contacting us or using the site, you understand that your information may be processed outside your country of residence.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
