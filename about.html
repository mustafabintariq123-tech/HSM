About

A research and analytics partner for deal teams

HSM Strategic exists to give lean deal teams the same research depth and modeling rigor as a fully staffed investment bank, without adding headcount.

Our Approach

Embedded, not outsourced in name only

We operate as a confidential extension of your team, working inside your process and formats rather than handing back generic output.

I.

Deal-Team Fluency

We work in the frameworks deal teams already use: CIMs, IC memos, comp sets, and diligence trackers, so our output drops straight into your process.

II.

Sector Depth

Particular depth in software and vertical market software transactions, alongside broad experience across industrial, business services, and healthcare deals.

III.

Discretion by Default

Every engagement is treated as confidential. We work under NDA as standard and disclose client relationships only with permission.

Why HSM Strategic Exists

Deal flow is uneven. Headcount doesn't have to match it.

Boutique banks, independent sponsors, and lower middle-market funds face the same problem: research and modeling demand spikes with deal flow, but a full-time analyst hire is a fixed cost against a variable pipeline. HSM Strategic was built to close that gap — institutional-grade research and modeling support, engaged only when a deal is live.
