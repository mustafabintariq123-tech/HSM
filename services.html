Our Services

From origination to investment committee

Engage us for a single workstream or across the full deal lifecycle. Every deliverable is built to sit directly inside your process.

Full Lifecycle Coverage

Six services, one continuous line of coverage across a transaction, from the first target list to the memo in front of your investment committee.

01 Origination 02 Screening 03 Research 04 Valuation 05 Diligence 06 IC Support
01 — Origination

Deal Sourcing & Origination

Scope

Target identification, buyer/seller mapping, and pipeline support for teams building or refreshing a deal pipeline.

  • Target lists built against defined size, sector, and geography criteria
  • Buyer and seller universe mapping
  • Outreach-ready contact and pipeline tracking support
02 — Screening

Screening & Preliminary Analysis

Scope

Target shortlisting, CIM analysis, and competitive benchmarking to narrow a pipeline to the opportunities worth pursuing.

  • CIM and teaser review with summary flagging of key issues
  • Shortlisting against investment criteria
  • Competitive and comparable-company benchmarking
03 — Research

Market & Industry Research

Scope

Market sizing, industry deep dives, and risk analysis to support the investment thesis behind a deal.

  • TAM / SAM / SOM sizing and growth analysis
  • Industry structure, competitive dynamics, and regulatory landscape
  • Thesis-specific risk factor identification
04 — Valuation

Financial Modeling & Valuation

Scope

DCF, comps, precedent transactions, LBO, and scenario modeling built to investment-committee standard.

  • Three-statement and LBO models with sensitivity and scenario cases
  • Comparable company and precedent transaction analysis
  • Discounted cash flow and standalone valuation support
05 — Diligence

Due Diligence Support

Scope

Financial and commercial diligence, data room review, and red-flag reporting during the confirmatory phase of a transaction.

  • Normalized EBITDA bridges and revenue quality analysis
  • Data room review and document tracking
  • Operational and commercial red-flag identification
06 — IC Support

Investment Memos & IC Materials

Scope

Memo drafting and investment committee presentation support that turns diligence findings into a clear recommendation.

  • Investment memo drafting aligned to your IC format
  • Supporting exhibits, comps, and sensitivity tables
  • Presentation-ready IC materials
