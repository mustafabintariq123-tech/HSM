Pricing

What This Typically Costs

Most single workstreams start at $1,000, scoped and quoted on a quick call.

Every engagement is a fixed, defined fee agreed before work starts: no hourly billing, no surprise scope creep. How far above that starting point a workstream lands depends on which of the six service lines it touches and how much ground it covers, from a single screening pass to a full modeling build.

What Counts As One Engagement

One engagement is one bounded, defined deliverable against a single service line, agreed and priced before work starts, typically delivered in one to two weeks, not an open-ended retainer across a full deal. For example, one engagement is:

  • Origination: a buyer or seller list built against defined size, sector, and geography criteria
  • Screening: a CIM review plus comp set on one target
  • Research: a TAM/SAM/SOM sizing and market structure writeup
  • Financial Modeling & Valuation: a three-statement or LBO model, or an EBITDA bridge plus sensitivity exhibit
  • Diligence: a red-flag review of one data room
  • IC Support: one investment memo drafted off findings already in hand

Following a deal continuously across several of these is several engagements, not one, and is priced and scoped as a bundle when that's what's needed.

Lighter Scope
A single deliverable, such as one comp set, one screening pass, or one memo, runs toward the lower end.
Fuller Scope
Modeling-heavy or diligence-heavy workstreams, or several service lines bundled together, run toward the upper end.
First-Engagement Guarantee

If the deliverable doesn't match the brief we agreed on, I revise it until it does, at no extra cost. This applies to your first engagement with HSM Strategic, since that's the point where trust hasn't been built yet and there's nothing on this site but my word for it.

It covers matching the scope set before work started, not requests added afterward. A comp set missing a comparable specified up front gets fixed. Turning that comp set into a full valuation model partway through is a new engagement, scoped and priced like any other.
