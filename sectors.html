Where We Go Deepest

We support deal teams across sectors. These are the areas where we bring the most pattern recognition to your deals.

Vertical Market Software

Vertical market software is our core sector. Our experience comes from underwriting software acquisitions in the Constellation Software ecosystem, where founder-owned, mission-critical niche software businesses are bought to hold for the long term.

These businesses do not behave like horizontal SaaS, and they should not be underwritten like it. Maintenance revenue, customer concentration, switching costs and legacy technology all need to be read differently. That is where most of our diligence frameworks come from.

Typical work

  • Screening founder-owned VMS targets against acquirer criteria
  • Revenue quality, maintenance base and retention analysis
  • Switching cost and customer concentration assessment
  • Normalized EBITDA bridges and deferred revenue analysis
  • Competitive landscape maps for niche verticals with thin public data

Related insights

  • Maintenance Revenue Is the Asset

Healthcare Services and Healthcare Software

Our healthcare work centers on behavioral health: independent practices, substance use disorder providers and the software platforms that serve them. It is a fragmented market with many founder-owned operators, which makes it attractive for buy-and-build strategies and hard to map from standard databases.

Typical work

  • Founder and owner contact lists for independent practices, state by state
  • Bottom-up market sizing anchored to federal facility data
  • Industry event and conference mapping
  • Screening practices and platforms for add-on potential

Education and Student Transportation Software

K-12 education software, including student transportation, routing and school payments, is a classic vertical software market. Customers are public school districts, sales cycles run on procurement calendars, and once a system is in place it rarely gets replaced. We have mapped vendors, researched district buyers and profiled companies across this space.

Typical work

  • Vendor landscape maps for K-12 payments, transportation and adjacent EdTech
  • District decision-maker research across the largest districts by enrollment
  • Company profiles and target screening for acquirers in the space
